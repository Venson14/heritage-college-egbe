Admissions

Simple steps, clear requirements

Everything you need to know to begin the process with confidence.

Admission Requirements

  • Passport photograph
  • Completed application form
  • Academic transcripts or report cards
  • Recommendation letters where applicable
  • Birth certificate or age proof
  • Health records and immunization documents
  • Application and enrollment fees

Admission Process

  • Submit your application online
  • Provide required documents and details
  • Attend an entrance examination
  • Complete assessments or interviews if needed
  • Receive your admission decision
  • Complete enrollment paperwork and pay the deposit
  • Join orientation and begin your academic journey
